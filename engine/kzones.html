Every fresh S&D David zone on M15 (BOS → candle A of the first A-B-C gap, the class video 6 rule). Kronos-base draws 16 paths of the next 50 M15 candles, each with its own high / low, and checks every candle: Touch = paths whose wick reaches candle A's wick (the entry), 50 % = paths reaching the dashed 50 % line, Stop = paths that CLOSE beyond candle A, Stop first = the stop came before any touch. Candle = the median candle (1–50) of the touch. Read only, no orders. Nearest zones are forecast first (~1 min per symbol on the GPU).

Test on PAST candles — does Kronos know if price will touch the zone?

Old S&D David zones (newest first, up to 6 per symbol). At the candle after each BOS, Kronos sees ONLY the candles up to there (the zone is worked out again on them, no peek) and forecasts 50 candles; then the 50 candles that REALLY came are checked. AUC: 0.5 = coin flip, 1.0 = always right about which zones get touched. The baseline uses no model at all: "a nearer zone is touched more often". Kronos is worth something only if it beats the baseline.
